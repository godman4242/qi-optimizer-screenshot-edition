Max-duration qi pills — 48 min handcrafted tested

Tested and confirmed at 48 minutes hand-crafted. All five are qi-duration maxers:

  1. 3× Nine Suns Flame Grass, 2× Moonlight Jade Leaf, 1× Starlight Dew Herb
  2. 3× Nine Suns Flame Grass, 2× Moonlight Jade Leaf, 1× Thousand Year Lotus
  3. 5× Nine Suns Flame Grass, 1× Moonlight Jade Leaf
  4. 5× Nine Suns Flame Grass, 1× Starlight Dew Herb
  5. 5× Nine Suns Flame Grass, 1× Thousand Year Lotus

Ratio is fixed. You can't swap the 2 moonlight / 1 starlight-or-lotus split around — flipping those counts breaks the pill.

Community report, 23/07, independently confirmed — #alchemy-forum

Tier 9 pills & recipe drift recipes go stale

Tier 9 pills exist, and their recipes change when the dev patches the game. Treat any recipe you find — including everything in this app — as suspect until you re-test it in-game.

Evidence: crafters reported “same attribute incorrect, same tier incorrect” failures while grinding to tier 9 on 4 Aug, immediately after reports that recipes had been quietly changed on 3 Aug.

Community reports, 03–04/08 — #alchemy-forum

Instant-qi pill recipes — from the official Trello not in this app's optimizer

These graded qi pills exist in-game but are not in the Pill Codex dataset, so the optimizer cannot plan them. Recipes as published on the official Trello:

  • Yin-Yang Reversal (Heavenly) — 1× Thousand Year Lotus, 2× Crimson Flame Mushroom, 3× Nine Suns Flame Grass. +292 min of Qi at Tier IX.
  • Void Condensation (Heavenly) — 6× Starlight Dew Herb. +18 min hired.
  • Earth Core Marrow (Imperfect) — 3× Thousand Year Lotus, 2× Moonlight Jade, 1× Nine Suns Flame Grass. +16 min hired.
  • Azure Cloud Elixir (Heavenly) — 3× Blue Wave Coral, 2× Nine Suns Grass, 1× Starlight Dew Herb. +4 min hired. An Imperfect variant (4× Blue Wave Coral, 2× Mountain Green Herb) gives +70 min at Tier IX.

Same caveat as always: recipes drift when the dev patches. Re-test in-game before mass-crafting.
